  NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • This is a front-end demo: there is no real authentication. The
    "password" is a shared demo value so the sign-in form behaves like a
    normal one. Any account signs in instantly.

  • You can also switch accounts at any time WITHOUT logging out, from the
    floating "⚡ Demo" button (bottom-left of every page) -> "Sign in as".

  • "Create account" on the sign-in page makes a brand-new empty account
    (shipper or transporter) so you can see the first-run/onboarding view.
    It does not affect the seeded accounts above.

  • To wipe everything back to this clean seeded state: open the "⚡ Demo"
    console -> "Reset & reseed demo data".
