How it works

Four steps. Roughly eleven minutes from posting to award.

Structure the load

Route, weight, truck type, body length, material flags and a pickup window. Structured input is what makes the bids comparable.

We geofence the alert

Only fleet owners whose base or current position sits inside their own service radius of your pickup get pinged. Nobody gets spammed.

They compete

Blind sealed bids, or an open reverse auction where the leading number is visible and gets undercut. Counter-offers stay on the platform.

You award and track

Compare on more than price, award, follow the checkpoints to POD, then rate punctuality, cargo safety and communication separately.

Multi-parameter comparison

The cheapest bid is rarely the cheapest job.

A ₹700 saving means nothing if the truck turns up a day late and two crates arrive scuffed. Every bid on CargoBid carries the operator's reliability score, their three-vector rating, fleet size, verification state and how fast they can physically reach your gate.

We compute a Best value ranking across all of it — and we show you the maths, so you can overrule us.

Why it holds together

Trust is the only asset a young marketplace has.

We are deliberately not the incumbents' network. Everyone bidding here is a local fleet owner, which means the platform has to do the work of establishing who is good. Four mechanisms carry that load.

See a transporter profile

Document verification

GST, PAN and vehicle RCs are uploaded and checked. Only operators with all three clear carry the badge — and a badge visibly beats ₹500.

Three-vector ratings

Punctuality, cargo safety and communication are rated separately. One blended star is useless; knowing someone is punctual but silent is not.

Reliability score

Computed from real platform behaviour — deliveries, on-time rate, cancellations after winning, no-shows. It moves whether you like it or not.

Negotiation on record

Counter-offers and chat sit against the bid they belong to. When the rate changed and why is never somebody's word against another's.

Return-load optimiser

The empty return leg is where fleets bleed.

A Jaipur truck delivers to Delhi and then either dead-heads home burning its own diesel, or sits for two days waiting on a broker to find something. Both outcomes cost the owner money.

  • Set your current city the moment you unload
  • Every load heading back toward your home base surfaces instantly
  • Because the return leg is already a sunk cost, these bids come in sharp
  • Shippers on that lane get rates nobody else can match
Delhi → Jaipur, 268 km ₹6,970 of empty running a single backhaul removes from one truck's month. Multiply that across six trucks and it is the difference between a working year and a bad one.
